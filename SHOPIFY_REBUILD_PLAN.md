# Sue's Holiday Creations — Premium Shopify Rebuild Plan

A complete plan to rebuild **Sue's Holiday Creations** as a warm, premium, family-owned,
giftable Christmas brand on Shopify. Built to reuse the real business facts from the
current Wix site while upgrading layout, pricing, copy, branding, SEO, and conversion.

---

## 1. What's Wrong With the Current Website (Honest Audit)

The current site (Wix) is functional but leaves a lot of money and trust on the table.

**Branding & Trust**
- No clear brand promise above the fold. A visitor can't tell in 3 seconds *what makes
  these special* (handmade, personalized, family keepsakes).
- No founder story / face. The strongest asset — "made by hand by Sue" — is hidden.
- No reviews, testimonials, or social proof. Handmade gifts live and die on trust.
- No logo system, no consistent color palette, no premium typography.

**Pricing (the biggest problem)**
- Trees priced **$28.99–$60.99** read as *craft-fair* pricing, not heirloom keepsake
  pricing. This trains customers to see the product as cheap and undervalues Sue's labor.
- No Good/Better/Best tiers to anchor value and lift average order value.
- No bundles, no add-ons, no gift packaging upsell — every order is a one-item order.

**Product Presentation**
- Generic product names ("Family of 5 Tree") with thin descriptions.
- No emotional/gift framing ("a keepsake your family rebuilds every December").
- Photos (typical of Wix DIY sites) likely flat, inconsistent lighting, no lifestyle shots.

**Conversion & UX**
- No announcement bar (urgency / free-shipping threshold / order-by date).
- No clear "How custom personalization works" flow — confusing for personalized products.
- Missing key pages: FAQ, Shipping & Local Pickup, dedicated Custom Order page, Reviews.
- Likely weak mobile experience; most holiday shoppers buy on phones.
- No email capture / no abandoned-cart recovery / no post-purchase upsell.

**SEO**
- Thin meta titles/descriptions; not targeting "personalized family Christmas tree,"
  "magnetic family ornament," "handmade keepsake," etc.

## 4. Best Shopify Theme Recommendation

**Primary recommendation: "Dawn" (free, Shopify's flagship Online Store 2.0 theme).**
- Fast, mobile-first, fully section-based, free, and easy for a non-technical owner to edit.
- Strong image presentation — perfect for a visual, handmade product.
- Built-in support for product options, which we'll need for personalization.

**If budget allows a paid theme ($300–$400 one-time), choose one of:**
- **Craft** (free, Shopify) — literally designed for makers/artisans; very warm.
- **Prestige** (paid) — luxury feel, great for premium positioning.
- **Warehouse / Impulse** (paid) — strong for promotions, bundles, and announcement bars.

**Verdict:** Start on **Dawn** or **Craft** (both free) so launch costs stay near zero,
then style with the palette/fonts above. Upgrade to **Prestige** only after revenue proves it.

**Must-add apps:**
- Product personalization: **Infinite Options** or **Hulk Product Options** (free tiers exist)
  for name/family-member fields.
- Reviews: **Judge.me** (free plan) or **Shopify Product Reviews**.
- Email/SMS: **Shopify Email** (free) + **Klaviyo** (free to 250 contacts) for abandoned cart.
- Bundles/upsells: **Shopify Bundles** (free, native) + **Frequently Bought Together** /
  **ReConvert** for post-purchase upsell.
- Local pickup: native Shopify Local Pickup (free).

## 14. FAQ Page

**How does personalization work?** After you order, send us the names you'd like on each magnet
(we'll email you, or use the order notes). We hand-letter everything and double-check spelling.

**How long until it ships?** Most orders ship in **3–5 business days**. During peak season
(late Nov–Dec), please order early — see our holiday cutoff dates.

**Will it arrive by Christmas?** Order by our posted **Christmas cutoff date** (typically
mid-December) for guaranteed holiday delivery. Need it fast? Email us first.

**Are these really handmade?** Yes — every piece is made by hand by Sue. No two are identical,
and that's the magic.

**What are the trees and magnets made of?** Every magnet is **hand-sculpted from oven-baked
clay** and hand-lettered by Sue, then fitted with a magnet so it holds firmly and rearranges
easily on your tree. Baking hardens the clay into a durable, long-lasting keepsake — and because
each piece is sculpted by hand, no two are ever exactly alike.

**Can I add more family members later?** Absolutely — order add-on magnets anytime and they'll
match your tree.

**Can I order a custom or extra-large family tree?** Yes! Visit our Custom Orders page.

**Do you offer local pickup?** Yes — choose **Local Pickup** at checkout if you're nearby.

**What's your return policy?** Because each piece is personalized, we can't accept returns on
custom items, but if anything arrives damaged or incorrect, we'll **replace it free**. Your
happiness is the whole point.

**How do I care for it?** Your magnets are sculpted from oven-baked clay, so they're hardened,
durable, and built to last. Just dust gently with a soft dry cloth and store them flat in their
box between seasons. With a little everyday care, your keepsake will last for generations.

## 20. Exact Shopify Build Steps

1. **Create the store.** Sign up at shopify.com → choose the **Basic** plan ($) → set store
   name "Sue's Holiday Creations" → set currency, address, and email.
2. **Buy/connect the domain.** Point **suesholidaycreations.net** to Shopify (Settings →
   Domains → Connect existing domain) or buy a new one. Set as primary; enable SSL.
3. **Pick the theme.** Online Store → Themes → add **Dawn** or **Craft** (free) → Publish.
4. **Brand the theme.** Theme settings → set colors (palette in §2), fonts (serif heading + sans
   body), upload logo + favicon.
5. **Set up navigation.** Online Store → Navigation → build the menu from the sitemap (§3) and
   the footer menu (Care, Returns, Privacy, Contact).
6. **Create collections.** Products → Collections → create the 5 collections from §8 (manual or
   automated by tag).
7. **Add products.** Products → Add product for each item in §9. Paste premium + short
   descriptions, set the **recommended new prices**, add SEO title/meta, assign collection,
   set inventory, add the "Personalized" / "Handmade" tags.
8. **Add personalization fields.** Install **Infinite Options** (or Hulk) → add text fields to
   each tree ("Name on Magnet 1," etc.) so customers submit names at checkout.
9. **Build bundles.** Install **Shopify Bundles** → create the 5 gift sets from §10 with bundle
   pricing.
10. **Set up upsells.** Install **ReConvert** (post-purchase) + a "frequently bought together"
    app → configure add-on magnet offers.
11. **Configure shipping.** Settings → Shipping → set rates, **free shipping over $75**, and
    enable **Local Pickup** with your address/hours.
12. **Set up payments.** Settings → Payments → enable **Shopify Payments** + PayPal + Shop Pay.
13. **Taxes & policies.** Settings → Taxes; generate Refund/Privacy/Terms policies (Shopify
    templates) and paste the custom Returns/Replacement wording from §14.
14. **Reviews & email.** Install **Judge.me**; set up **Shopify Email** + **Klaviyo** welcome,
    abandoned-cart, and post-purchase flows; add the homepage email-capture popup (10% off).
15. **Announcement bar.** Theme editor → add the announcement bar, paste rotating copy (§18).
16. **Build the homepage** in the theme editor using sections in §5; paste copy from §6.
17. **Build static pages.** Online Store → Pages → create About, FAQ, Shipping & Local Pickup,
    Contact, Custom Orders (use a form app like **Shopify Forms** for the custom request form).
18. **Add photos.** Upload the new photography per the shot list (§21) — hero, lifestyle, macro,
    gift-box, and founder shots.
19. **Mobile QA.** Preview on mobile; check tap targets, image load, and checkout flow.
20. **Test an order.** Place a test order (Bogus Gateway or a real $ test) to confirm
    personalization fields, shipping, pickup, and email flows fire correctly.
21. **Launch.** Remove password protection (Online Store → Preferences), submit sitemap to
    Google Search Console, set the meta/social preview image, and announce on Facebook.

## 21. Photography Shot List (what to shoot — current photos are likely the weak point)

For a premium feel, replace DIY snapshots with:
1. **Hero lifestyle** — a finished family tree on a styled mantel with warm bokeh lights,
   garland, soft daylight. (Homepage hero.)
2. **Clean product-on-white** — every product shot on a clean cream/white background, consistent
   angle and lighting, for the shop grid.
3. **Macro detail** — extreme close-ups of the hand-sculpted clay texture and hand-lettered
   names (the visible fingerprints-of-the-maker shots are what *sell* "handmade clay").
4. **Scale shot** — product in-hand so size is obvious.
5. **Gift-box presentation** — the keepsake in/next to premium packaging (justifies gift pricing).
6. **In-use / human** — a child or grandparent placing a magnet; hands arranging the tree.
7. **Founder/workshop** — Sue making a piece; her hands, tools, workspace (trust + story).
8. **Family-size comparison** — a row showing 2 → 8 so shoppers self-select.
9. **UGC** — real customers' trees in their homes (collect via review/email requests).

**Tips:** shoot in consistent warm natural light, same background per category, square (1:1) for
grid + a few vertical (4:5) for mobile, and keep editing consistent (slightly warm, not oversaturated).
