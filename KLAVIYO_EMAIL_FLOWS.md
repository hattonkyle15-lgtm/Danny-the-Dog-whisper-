# Sue's Holiday Creations — Klaviyo Email Flows

Ready-to-build automated flows. Copy is written in Sue's warm, family-owned, handmade voice.
Each flow lists the **trigger**, **timing**, and **email-by-email copy** (subject, preview text,
body). Build each in Klaviyo → Flows → Create Flow.

> Setup notes: Connect Klaviyo to Shopify (native integration). Set the sender name to
> "Sue's Holiday Creations" and reply-to SuesHolidayCreations@gmail.com. Keep emails simple,
> warm, and mobile-first — one clear button per email. Use the evergreen/gold/cream palette.

## Flow 2 — Abandoned Cart

**Trigger:** Checkout Started / Added to Cart, and not Placed Order.
**Goal:** Recover the sale; reassure about handmade/personalization.

### Email 2.1 — +1 hour: Friendly nudge
- **Subject:** You left a little something behind 🎁
- **Preview:** Your handmade keepsake is waiting.
- **Body:**
  Hi! It looks like you were building a keepsake with us — it's still in your cart.
  Every piece is made and personalized by hand, so yours is ready whenever you are.
  **[ Return to My Cart → ]**
  Questions before you buy? Just reply — a real person (usually me!) reads every message. — Sue

### Email 2.2 — +24 hours: Handle objections + social proof
- **Subject:** Still thinking it over?
- **Preview:** Handmade, personalized, and shipped with care.
- **Body:**
  Just in case it helps:
  • **Hand-sculpted from clay** — every magnet is shaped and lettered by hand, so no two are alike.
  • **Personalized free** — just send us your family's names.
  • **Ships in 3–5 days**, carefully packed and ready to gift.
  • **Made by hand in the USA** — no two are exactly alike.
  *"It made my mom cry — every grandkid's name was on it." — a happy customer*
  **[ Finish My Order → ]**

### Email 2.3 — +48 hours: Light incentive (optional)
- **Subject:** A little something to help you decide
- **Preview:** Here's 10% off to complete your keepsake.
- **Body:**
  I'd love to make your family's keepsake. Here's **10% off** to finish up:
  **Code: KEEPSAKE10**
  Handmade in small batches — order soon so I have time to make yours with care.
  **[ Complete My Order → ]**
  *(Tip: only add the discount to the 3rd email so you're not training shoppers to abandon.)*

## Flow 4 — Post-Purchase / Thank You + Personalization Reminder

**Trigger:** Placed Order.
**Goal:** Confirm warmly, collect personalization details, set expectations, upsell add-ons.

### Email 4.1 — Immediate: Thank you + send us your names
- **Subject:** Thank you! Now, let's personalize it ❤️
- **Preview:** Just send us the names for your keepsake.
- **Body:**
  Thank you so much for your order — it truly means the world to a small family business.
  To personalize your keepsake, **reply to this email with the names** you'd like on each
  magnet (and let us know any spellings to double-check!).
  I'll hand-make and ship your order within **3–5 business days**.
  With gratitude,
  Sue
  *P.S. Want to add a pet paw or extra name? Just mention it in your reply — I'll send a quick
  invoice for the add-on.*

### Email 4.2 — +5 days: Add-on upsell
- **Subject:** Room for one more on your tree?
- **Preview:** Add a magnet anytime — it'll match perfectly.
- **Body:**
  Families grow — and your keepsake is made to grow with them.
  Anytime you welcome a new baby, partner, grandchild, or pet, you can add a handmade magnet
  that matches your tree perfectly.
  **[ Shop Add-On Magnets → ]**
- **Button link:** /collections/add-on-magnets

### Email 4.3 — +10 days: Review request
- **Subject:** How did your keepsake turn out?
- **Preview:** I'd love to see it — and a quick review helps so much.

  I hope you love your handmade keepsake as much as I loved making it!

  it makes my day.

## Flow 6 — Holiday Cutoff Urgency (Broadcast Campaigns, not automated)

Send as one-off campaigns to your whole list in December:
- **~Dec 8:** "One week left to order for Christmas delivery 🎄"
- **~Dec 14:** "Last call — order by tomorrow for Christmas (handmade to order)"
- **~Dec 16+ (switch to pickup/digital):** "Missed the cutoff? Local pickup is still available"

---

## Segments to Create in Klaviyo

1. **Newsletter subscribers (non-buyers)** — for welcome + nurture.
2. **First-time customers** — for post-purchase + winback.
3. **Repeat customers / VIPs** — early access to limited holiday drops.
4. **Engaged in last 90 days** — for browse/abandon flows.
5. **Past seasonal buyers (1+ year ago)** — for the November winback.

## Quick KPIs to watch
- Welcome flow → first-purchase conversion
- Abandoned cart recovery rate (target 5–10%+)
- Post-purchase add-on attach rate
- Review submission rate
- November winback repeat-purchase rate
